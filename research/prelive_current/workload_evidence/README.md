# Workload diagnosis evidence, 2026-10-06

These are bounded functional engineering probes, not host cost runs. There is no
Windows QPC, capture/RPC, device input, emulator or physical gold in this folder.
No original STOP campaign was reopened and no original scene or oracle changed.
The final supported reproducer is `../cost_workload_tests.cpp`, using the separate
portable validation lane. The prototype source/results remain as negative evidence.

## Order and exact scope

1. `v3_workload_probe.cpp/.log`: four predeclared fill/exterior combinations over
   the 12 historical Hold positions. The original cyan+dark30 has zero body,
   front, contact, relation or Down eligibility. Blue alone repairs body/contact
   but not front. Black alone does not repair cyan. Both repair the v3 query
   contract (four eligible positions). These are explicit synthetic queries,
   not a full observer/owner or a physical opportunity denominator.
2. `runtime_workload_probe.cpp/.json`: 120 actual RGB observer/owner attempts for
   historical Hold, the first proposed shared stimulus and historical Tap, A/B.
   Historical and first proposed Hold both have zero actions. Tap has 1 Down/Up
   per variant. The first draft `cost_workload_tests-first.cpp` therefore failed
   its shared-action-positive assertions; the failure remains in
   `../../prelive_portable/evidence/release-01/cost_workload_tests.json`.
3. `runtime_front_probe.cpp`, three JSON results and three header snapshots:
   isolate the near-line front measurement. First head-at-frame5=500 selects
   the white judgment-line terminal and gives neither A nor B Down. Head=502
   gives B entry only. Head=503 gives A/B entry and later safety Up. Prototype
   name `hold-shared-v1` was withdrawn, never integrated or admitted to the cost
   CLI. The first, second and third headers correspond to 500, 502 and 503.
4. `workload_sweep.cpp` and `workload-sweep.json`: before execution the sweep was
   fixed at all integer head-at-frame5 positions 490..510, both variants, then
   stationary body/leave/absence/effect at 503, 12 frames each. All 50 results
   and 600 frame attempts remain. `workload-sweep-summary.json` includes all
   successful and zero-action cases plus every injection phase receipt.
   Common entry exists at only one phase, 503, and no normal tail completion
   was established. Parameter search stopped after this declared sweep.

The first direct full-closure link attempt failed due to unused game-analysis
references (`pas::distribution`, `pas::sha256_file`); adding function/data sections
and linker garbage collection removed unreachable analysis/device dependencies.
That first linker diagnostic was captured by the task tool, not redirected to a
file. The later empty `.compile.log` is the successful compile's stderr/stdout,
not a claim that the first link succeeded.

The first direct Runtime probe compiled against official nlohmann v3.12.0.
The front probes and sweep reused the portable release-01 archive built against
the restored, exact historical Windows JSON patch. Exact hashes are in
`dependency-and-archive-bindings.sha256`; the original closure hashes are in
`original-closure-sources.sha256`. The release-01 archive predates the parallel
schema-4 diagnostic serialization addition in `src/game.cpp`; no action policy
was changed. Final three-mode tests rebuild/reuse each mode's current closure.

`SHA256SUMS` is checked from the repository root. It binds the preserved files,
not a Windows Job/identity/held/streams execution receipt. The copied pre-sweep
source receipt retains its original diagnostic output path; the source content
is also independently bound by the relocatable index.

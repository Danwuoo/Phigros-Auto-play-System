# Additional tail-only controls requested during independent review

The original TAIL_ONLY_REVIEW_PLAN and its SHA remain unchanged. These are additive
controls, not relaxed or replaced expectations. Original failures remain preserved.

1. After two typed tail witnesses schedule an Up, a fresh ordinary shifted body or
   returned/rotated body cannot regain Move permission, renew evidence or retarget
   the release. This restriction applies only to the new terminal-only closing path.
2. An incoming complete snapshot with two same-note targets is ambiguous for typed
   terminal acceptance: identical tail/tail, contradictory tail/tail and tail/body
   combinations must not confirm a terminal. The dedicated path must reset its
   unconfirmed history rather than let one valid duplicate dominate a contradiction.
3. A confirmed closing identity keeps its exact Up+20ms under the original body100ms
   bound even if removing its terminal-only target from ordinary visibility would
   otherwise trip missing-body60ms. An unconfirmed witness keeps the original60ms
   missing rule. Gate/context/fault revocation still supersedes normal closing.
4. Unknown Up from the typed closing path stays unknown, performs no Down/Up retry,
   and retains the first safety-release evidence.
5. Direct typed targets with extreme current timestamps fail closed. The independent
   fixture avoids signed arithmetic overflow; this is not a centuries-long runtime.
6. Transport cannot be downgraded into ordinary body permission. Actual observer
   descriptions can carry held_body_evidence=true even when terminal-only transport
   grants no body permission. With that flag, test missing enum, wrong enum and both
   enum/payload removed while the reserved tail-only reason remains. All must reject
   the inconsistent typed transport before ordinary alias/body/birth processing.
